### Known gotchas & guardrails
- **Don't invent facts.** Use only the cv.forret.com and LinkedIn-activity content in the Analysis. DCProof: 2026–now (started summer 2026); describe it briefly and link to `../portfolio/dcproof.md` for details.
- **Privacy:** no full birth date, family details or MBTI anywhere, including front matter `description` and `llms.txt` (which copies pages).
- **Never run `mkdox2 build`** (it commits and pushes, which publishes). Build with `docker run --platform linux/amd64 --rm -v "$PWD":/docs pforret/mkdox-zensical build`. Don't commit unless asked.
- Front matter must be on line 1 of every page (gotcha recorded in `docs/todo/decisions/gotchas.md`).
- `nav` is hand-tuned: change only the About entry. `.pages` files are ignored.
- The editorial design says no emoji in the UI: use `:material-…:`/`:octicons-…:` icons, not `:smile:`-style emoji.
- The timeline CSS must work in **both** `[data-md-color-scheme="default"]` and `"slate"`; use the `--ed-*` and `--md-*` variables, never hard-coded black or white.
- With `navigation.indexes`, `about/index.md` is the section page of the About tab (like `portfolio/index.md`).
- Keep `extra.css` changes in a clearly commented `/* ---- timeline ---- */` block; don't touch the rest.

- **About:** a short first-person bio from the headline and specialisations, the photo, location and availability, e-mail and profile links, and three grid cards (CV, Timeline, Portfolio).

- **Config:** `nav` About becomes a three-page list.
- **Home page:** `docs/index.md` gets the headline intro and links.
- **Docs:** `CLAUDE.md` gets a line on the About structure.
