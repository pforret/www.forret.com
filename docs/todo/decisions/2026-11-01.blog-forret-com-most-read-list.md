# blog.forret.com most-read list is curated, without joke posts

## Context
Analytics showed the most-read posts, including joke posts ("Top Ten Smelly Celebrities", "Size doesn't matter") and a reprinted legal-threat email ("Govern yourselves accordingly").

## Options considered
- Top 10 exactly as ranked: true to the numbers, includes the joke posts
- Curated top 10: drop the joke and off-tone posts, fill up from the next ones
- Grouped by theme instead of ranked

## Decision
blog.forret.com most-read list is curated, without joke posts

## Consequences
The list isn't a pure ranking. "The Mythical Agent-Month" (#12) was moved up to replace "Govern yourselves accordingly" (a default chosen during planning). No view counts are shown, so the list can be refreshed without implying exact numbers.
