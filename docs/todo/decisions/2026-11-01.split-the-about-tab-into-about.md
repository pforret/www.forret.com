# Split the About tab into About, CV and Timeline pages

## Context
The About section has to carry a bio, a full CV and a career overview.

## Options considered
- About + CV + Timeline: clear entry points for clients and recruiters
- About + CV: the timeline as a list at the top of the CV
- A single page: simplest, but long

## Decision
Split the About tab into About, CV and Timeline pages

## Consequences
The `nav` About entry is a three-page list. New about pages must be added there.
