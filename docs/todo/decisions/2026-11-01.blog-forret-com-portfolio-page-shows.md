# blog.forret.com portfolio page shows only AI and tech quotes

## Context
The item asked for "some quotes" from blog.forret.com on its portfolio page. Planning had to choose which lines, since the blog mixes AI essays, tech how-tos and humour.

## Options considered
- All five candidates (tagline, "send me a link", two Mythical Agent-Month lines, PrintThatVideo): more range and humour, less professional
- Only the AI/tech ones (Mythical Agent-Month x2, PrintThatVideo): fits the consultant portfolio, loses the blog's humour
- Peter supplies his own favourites: most personal, but blocks the item

## Decision
blog.forret.com portfolio page shows only AI and tech quotes

## Consequences
The page sounds serious and current (AI-assisted development). Quotes are copied exactly as published. If more quotes are added later, they should also come from the AI/tech posts.
