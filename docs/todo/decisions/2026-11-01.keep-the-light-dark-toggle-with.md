# Keep the light/dark toggle with an inverted ink/cream variant of the editorial design

## Context
The vintage editorial design is light-only, but the user had chosen a light/dark toggle in planning.

## Options considered
- Light only, as in the design
- Keep the toggle with an ink background and cream type in `slate` (done)

## Decision
Keep the light/dark toggle with an inverted ink/cream variant of the editorial design

## Consequences
Every new style in `extra.css` must define or inherit colours for both `default` and `slate`.
