# Show timeline years as block labels without a separator

## Context
The PRP said timeline items start with a bold year range followed by the text, and the first draft used " · " as separator.

## Options considered
- "**2006–now** · text": a stray dot under the block-level year label
- "**2006–now** text" (done): the year label on its own line

## Decision
Show timeline years as block labels without a separator

## Consequences
New timeline entries must follow the `- **YYYY–YYYY** text` pattern without a separator.
