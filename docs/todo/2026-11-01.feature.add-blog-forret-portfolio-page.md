## Analysis
New portfolio entry: blog.forret.com is Peter's personal blog since 2004 (already linked from
`about/cv.md`, `about/timeline.md`, `about/index.md` and the social links in `zensical.toml`), but it
has no portfolio card or page of its own.

The page should present the blog (20+ years, technology and culture), show a few quotes pulled
from the posts, and list the most popular posts from the screenshot above with their real titles
and full URLs.

Open questions:
- The slugs in the screenshot are truncated: the full URLs and titles must be looked up on
  blog.forret.com (sitemap or RSS) during implementation.
- "Some quotes": quotes picked from the popular posts by Claude, or does Peter have favourite lines?
  Proposal: 3–5 short pull quotes from the popular posts, shown as blockquotes, Peter to approve.
- Skip `/`, `/about`, `/category/idea` and the duplicate ayurveda/celebrities paths (old URL forms)
  from the "popular posts" list; show the top ~10 distinct posts, without view counts.

## Impacted files
- `docs/portfolio/blog.md`: new page (intro, quotes, popular posts, link to the blog)
- `docs/portfolio/index.md`: new card in the portfolio grid
- `zensical.toml`: add the page to `nav` under Portfolio (hand-tuned block)

## Plan
1. Fetch blog.forret.com's sitemap/RSS and resolve each truncated path to its full URL and title.
2. Read the top posts and pick 3–5 short quotes; confirm the selection with Peter.
3. Write `docs/portfolio/blog.md` in the style of the other flagship pages (`dcproof.md`,
   `toolstudio.md`): intro, quotes as blockquotes, "Most read" list, CTA button to the blog.
4. Add a card to `docs/portfolio/index.md` and the page to `nav` in `zensical.toml`.
5. Test build with the plain `docker run … build` command and check `site/portfolio/blog/`.

## Acceptance criteria
- `/portfolio/blog/` exists, linked from the Portfolio overview and in the navigation.
- It shows 3–5 quotes from blog posts and ~10 most popular posts with working full URLs and titles.
- The test build finishes without warnings about the new page.
