CV¶

Peter Forret: CTO, software architect · Grimbergen, Belgium · born 1969 in Roeselare · peter@forret.com · LinkedIn

Since 2024 focused on A.I.: A.I.-assisted software development (Claude Code and coding agents) and A.I. for media processing (video, speech and language models for quality control, age rating, news analysis and forecasting).

Specialised in A.I. (audio/video quality control, forecasting, MCP services); (web) application architecture, product requirements and technical writing; microservices, DevOps, automation, PKI and security; audio, video and image processing. I take products from product management and software architecture all the way to implementation.
