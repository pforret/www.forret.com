# DCProof

**Role:** Founder · **Website:** [www.dcproof.net](<https://www.dcproof.net>)

[![DCProof](<https://www.forret.com/portfolio/img/dcproof.png>)](<https://www.forret.com/portfolio/img/dcproof.png>)

DCProof is an automated, AI-powered quality-control and content-control service for short-form media. Submit a spot or trailer, and minutes later you get a report: every frame and every second of audio is analysed.

It covers cinema, TV, out-of-home, radio, social media ads (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok) and film trailers.

## Products

**[DCProof QC Report](<https://www.dcproof.net/products/qc-report/>)**

A quality- and content-control report for each video, delivered as a PDF and as a JSON data blob. Videos are submitted through the API or a submit-URL.

**[DCProof Rating Tool](<https://www.dcproof.net/products/rating-tool/>)**

A web app for age rating. Reviewers jump between timecoded moments and check them against market standards such as FSK, BBFC, Kijkwijzer and GoedGezien, then record their decision.
