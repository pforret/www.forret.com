# Four visions of the AI decade

[![Four visions of the AI decade](<https://www.forret.com/blog/posts/img/four-visions-of-the-ai-decade.jpg>)](<https://www.forret.com/blog/posts/img/four-visions-of-the-ai-decade.jpg>)

> If you work in software, you've probably had the same weird couple of years I've had. You ask an LLM to fix a bug, it fixes the bug. You ask your coding agent to improve the architecture and performance of a complete app, and it does just that. Somewhere in the back of your head a voice asks: okay, but where does this actually go?

At some point I got tired of getting my answer to that question from tweet threads and podcast hot takes, so I went to the source material: four documents and essay series with numbers, dates and claims that can be checked against 2026.

| Vision | Source | In one line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Situational Awareness** | Leopold Aschenbrenner, June 2024 | AGI around 2027 from straight-line trends; *win* the race |
| **AI 2027** | Kokotajlo, Alexander, Larsen, Lifland, Dean, April 2025 | A month-by-month scenario with two endings; *look closely* before accelerating |
| **Machines of Loving Grace** &amp; **The Adolescence of Technology** | Dario Amodei, 2024–2026 | "A country of geniuses in a datacenter"; *steer* it |
| **The Intelligence Age**, **The Gentle Singularity** &amp; **Abundant Intelligence** | Sam Altman, 2024–2025 | Superintelligence a few thousand days away; *build* it |

In the full post I summarise each vision in ten key points, compare the two scenario documents, put all four side by side, and do a **reality check for mid-2026**: what's on track (coding agents, data center capex), what isn't yet, and how far behind the scenarios we actually are.

The short version: the direction of travel hasn't been falsified on any major axis, but the timing seems to run later than the scenarios projected.
